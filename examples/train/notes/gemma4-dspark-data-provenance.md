Both DSpark training corpora are on-policy (assistant turns written by the target,
gemma-4-31B-it). Verified 2026-10-12 by hashing prompts/answers across the arrow shards.

- 349k = /sms-scratch/mengmengj/data/gemma4_dspark (349,138 rows): kimi-mtp-dataset
  prompts, answers regenerated by gemma-4-31B-it (chenw's
  kimi-regen-gemma4-31b/train_regen.jsonl; scripts/response_regeneration/
  regenerate_gemma4_31b_all_gpus.sh). Original Kimi-K2.5 answers dropped.
- 782k = /sms-scratch/mengmengj/data/kimi_nemotron_782k -> symlinks to
  /sms-scratch/ravira/datasets/kimi-mtp-nemotron-stem-code-math (782,413 rows, built
  2026-08-11 by Ravi). Composition: ~291k kimi-mtp prompts (273k byte-identical to our
  Gemma regen; the rest same-style Gemma regen from a different pass) + ~491k Nemotron-v2
  stem/math/code prompts regenerated by gemma-4-31B-it on 2026-07-30..08-01
  (/sms-scratch/ravira/speculators/output/nemotron_v2/nemotron-v2__{stem,math,code}
  _regen_gemma-4-31B-it_.jsonl, single-turn, no <think> traces; regen script strips
  R1 reasoning and regenerates).
- All rows single-turn user->assistant, no system prompts. [[dspark-run-recipe]]
